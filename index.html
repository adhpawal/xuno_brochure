The four layers

Layer 01 · The Backbone
Infrastructure Layer

"Think of this as the plumbing — the pipes, valves, and vault door that make sure money actually moves and lands safely, every single time."

  • Three direct bank partners — Priority Commerce in the US, plus two banks in Nepal — extended by a correspondent network that reaches further
  • Plug-and-play corridor model: any originator with BaaS/origination capability, and any receiver with a remittance or applicable license, can be connected and activated fast
  • One shared ledger keeps track of every currency and every transaction, in real time
  • Onboarding: sign-up, ID checks, and instant account linking — customers are ready to transact in minutes
  • Products: delivery-speed tiers, subscriptions, and a live FX calculator, ready to plug in
  • Five ready-made APIs — Payment/Payout, Virtual Account, Notifications, Reconciliation, KYC Screening — built as small, independent services that ship fast

Layer 02 · The Vault
Treasury Layer

"This is the safe where everyone's money waits — guaranteed to be there before it's sent, and one day, a safe that can grow your money too."

  • Every payout is checked and guaranteed before it's sent — no surprises mid-transfer

Under the hood

The APIs actually doing the work.

Seven endpoints, each its own microservice with one specific job. Plug into just one, or all seven, depending on how deep you want to integrate.

Payment / Payout API
⚙ Payments Service
Does
Sends money out — single transfers, batch runs, or scheduled payouts.
Powers
Every outbound embedded finance transaction and bulk business payroll run.
Virtual Account / IBAN API
⚙ Accounts Service
Does
Issues a real, bank-grade account number in seconds.
Powers
Onboarding, sending-entity & destination-entity detail for cross-border payments and banking, plus XVA balances.
Reconciliation API
⚙ Reconciliation Service
Does
Matches every incoming payment to the right customer, automatically.
Powers
Same-day settlement confidence and a clean audit trail.
Notification API
⚙ Notifications Service
Does
Pushes real-time status updates into partner systems.
Powers
Live dashboards, delivery-speed tracking, instant customer alerts.
KYC Sanctions Screening API
⚙ Compliance Service
Does
Screens every customer & transaction against sanctions lists in real time.
Powers
Safe, compliant onboarding at scale — no manual review queues.
Ledger API
⚙ Ledger Service
Does
Exposes real-time balances and full transaction history, in every currency.
Powers
One source of truth for every account — reconciled and audit-ready by design.
FX Rates API
⚙ Pricing & FX Service
Does
Returns live, competitor-checked exchange rates for every corridor pair.
Powers
Accurate pricing at checkout, with rates that move with the market.

Use cases

Why partners actually choose Xuno.

Xuno's correspondent banking layer plugs in any originator with BaaS/origination capability and any receiver with a remittance or applicable license — activating new corridors fast, whoever is building.

Money Transfer Operator
A new corridor, live in weeks with integration.

An MTO wants to add a receive corridor it isn't licensed in. It plugs into Xuno's Payment/Payout & FX Rates APIs — Xuno's correspondent banking layer connects it to an already-licensed receiving partner, so the corridor goes live in weeks with integration, not months.

Money Service Business
Regulated accounts, no new license.

An MSB wants to hold customer balances and offer multi-currency accounts without pursuing a new license. It white-labels Xuno's Virtual Account & Ledger APIs and launches bank-grade accounts under its own brand, backed by Xuno's existing licenses and bank partners.

Fintech / Neobank
Embedded cross-border, without the overhead.

A neobank wants customers to send money home, right inside its own app. Instead of negotiating bank relationships and compliance from scratch, it integrates Xuno's APIs directly and ships a compliant, branded cross-border feature in weeks.

Bank · in the pipeline
BaaS, without building it in-house.

A bank wants to offer banking-as-a-service to its own fintech and business customers, without building the infrastructure. It partners with Xuno for the compliant rails, ledger, and APIs — and offers BaaS under its own name.
